What you get

  • One-click install & updates

    Builds come straight from each project's own GitHub releases, checked and kept current.

  • Playable status per OS

    See at a glance whether a port is perfect, playable or still in progress on your system.

  • Cloud saves you own

    Save sync through a folder your own iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive keeps in step.

  • Sign in with Bluesky

    Use any AT Protocol account. Your profile, library and reviews stay portable.

  • Discussion boards & chat

    A board for every game, plus friends, DMs and invites to play together.

  • Steam Deck ready

    Couch mode with large tiles and full gamepad navigation.

A look inside

Games, stars, statuses, releases and pictures are from the live catalog; game pictures come from the awesome-decomp-games list and the ports' own GitHub pages. The people and conversations are examples.

  • Install and play in one click

  • Your library

  • Discussions for every game

    Ask for help, report bugs and share mods. Threads are Bluesky posts.

  • Friends and invites

    Message friends over Bluesky and invite them to play.

How it works

  1. 1

    Find a port

    Browse community ports by game, status and platform.

  2. 2

    Add your game files

    They're checked on your device and never uploaded.

  3. 3

    Play

    Portside installs the build and launches it for you.

  4. 4

    Your saves follow you

    Pick up on another device where you left off.

Built on the community's work

Portside lists projects built by their own teams. The catalog starts from awesome-decomp-games, and every listing links back to the port's maintainers and source. Portside never hosts or links to ROMs or game assets.
